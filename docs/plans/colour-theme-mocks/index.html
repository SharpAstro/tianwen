Colour core candidates

Three cores, each in Light / Dark / Night, drawn as the same slice of the GUI so they are comparable.

text/bg and error/bg are WCAG contrast ratios (4.5:1 is AA for body text). rod is scotopic stimulation, averaged over the four foreground colours, weighted by V'(lambda) at each sRGB primary's dominant wavelength. It is what Night mode exists to minimise: lower is darker-adapted. Red is roughly 30 to 40 times cheaper than green or blue, so any G or B in a Night palette is a cost paid for hue separation.
