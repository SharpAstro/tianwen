Archive Bake Masters

Every session master in D:/Astro-Dataset/2026-09-19-full, the full-archive bake. Cards show each master cropped to its covered rectangle and enhanced; open one for the same crop before enhancement, on the same white balance, and a 1:1 patch of sky from the uncropped master. A night the mount flipped through appears three times — both sides together, then each side — which the badges tell apart.

Both views of a card share one colour calibration. The unenhanced view is rendered first and its white balance is handed to the enhanced one, rather than each solving its own SPCC against a background the enhance has already flattened. A card tagged mono has one channel and so no colour to calibrate; one tagged uncalibrated colour could not be plate-solved, so the sky was neutralised instead and the signal keeps the sensor's own balance. A dual-narrowband master (L-Ultimate, L-Quad) reads teal on purpose: OIII lands on both the green and the blue photosites, so those two channels carry the same signal and there is no honest curve to fit.
