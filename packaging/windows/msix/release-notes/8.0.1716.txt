The MSIX comes from a DISPATCH, never from a push. The msix job is gated on
github.event_name == 'workflow_dispatch', so neither merging to main nor opening a
PR produces one: the merge of #260 raised run 1715 and built no package at all.
Run 1716 is the dispatch that did, which is where the 1716 in the version comes
from -- the patch segment is github.run_number and is never written by hand.

This file is the submission record for one Store release: the copy pasted into
Partner Center's "What's new in this version" field, and the detail behind it.
Nothing else in the repo records what has been submitted, which is why writing
it down is worth the file.

First MAJOR bump. The reason is API, not features: TianWen.Lib lost a parameter
from GetLumaStretchStatsAsync, StretchParameters.Presets became an ImmutableArray
and Default a property over it, FindStarsAsync took a SpikeGuard before its
CancellationToken, and the dependency floor moved to DIR.Lib 9.0. Per the org
convention (../.github/CLAUDE.md, "Versioning") that is X.* -> (X+1).0. None of
that is visible to a Store user, so the WHAT'S NEW block below says nothing about
it; what IS visible is that three things RENDER differently, which is the half of
a major no signature announces.

TWO CLAIMS IN 7.1.1627's LISTING ARE NOW WRONG, AND ONE WAS WRONG WHEN WRITTEN
================================================================================
Both are live in the Store listing right now, so the block below corrects them
rather than merely adding to them.

1. "Press O for a sky map drawn behind the image ... O steps through grid,
   objects, then full sky." The sky left the O ladder for its own key, Y
   (a44518e9). O is now the whole annotation, on or off, and Shift+O walks the
   ladder backwards. A user following the current listing presses O three times
   and never reaches the sky.

2. "The ring traces the object's true axis ratio and position angle, so a tilted
   galaxy like M31 rings as the disc it is." It did not. ComputeScreenPA carried
   TWO independent reflections (07eb16e3): a compass angle handed to a consumer
   expecting a math angle, and a screen-Y negation for a flip that
   WcsAnnotationLayer.ImageToScreen does not perform. Measured against NGC 206, a
   star cloud lying ON M31's major axis and therefore an independent check, the
   axis drew at bearing 128.6 degrees where 51.4 is correct. It was plausible at
   some angles and correct at none, which is how it passed review. The claim is
   true as of this release; it was not true when it was submitted.

   Worth knowing for next time: the two tests covering it asserted only
   float.IsFinite, which no reflection can fail. A check that re-derives its
   expectation through the convention under test cannot see a reflection in that
   convention.

----- BEGIN WHAT'S NEW -----
Your targets are brighter, and faint detail actually shows
The default auto stretch is now an inspection stretch: the sky sits where N.I.N.A.'s preview puts it, and structure just above the noise separates far enough to see. Colour frames also stop showing fine stripes across the background, and stop aliasing when zoomed out to fit.

The sky behind your photograph moved to Y
It used to be the third press of O. O is now the whole annotation, grid and markers together, and Shift+O steps back through it.

Every frame in the file list gets its own stretch
Clicking through a folder used to apply the first frame's curve to all the rest, so a galaxy went dim as the sky got darker. Ctrl+H holds one mapping across frames when you do want to compare, and a blink holds on its own.

Also in this release
- Object rings now sit at the correct angle, so a tilted galaxy rings as the disc it is.
- Ctrl+O opens a file, Ctrl+S saves the clean 16-bit raster, Ctrl+Shift+S opens the save menu.
- W opens white balance, with the colour calibration inside it.
- A held frame is marked [H] in the file list, and Shift+H steps the HDR view back.
- Auto crop no longer mistakes a dead pixel for the edge of the stack.
- One-shot-colour frames report three colours in the histogram and statistics.
- FITS thumbnails no longer download your OneDrive files just to look at them.
- The deep-sky catalogue is refreshed to OpenNGC v20260501.
----- END WHAT'S NEW -----

What renders differently (the half of a major no signature announces)
  4188974f  The default auto stretch is an inspection stretch, N.I.N.A.'s
            per-light preview exactly: the sky lands at 0.2 rather than 0.1, and
            a pixel three MAD above it separates by more than 0.08 where the old
            default gave 0.036. That is why a target could be barely visible here
            while N.I.N.A. showed it plainly on the same sub, and the 150 percent
            Boost could not make up the difference -- 0.1 x 1.5 is still under
            0.2 before contrast enters. Pinned on the RENDERED result rather than
            the parameters, since what went wrong was never that the numbers
            looked unusual.
  a8d59a29  Every VNG demosaic changes. A gradient in a demosaic compares two
            samples of the SAME colour, so it is zero on a flat field whatever
            the sky's colour is; VNG's were colour differences AND an affine
            function of the value they select, so "keep the smallest gradient"
            meant "keep the value nearest the centre pixel's own level". Green
            read about +99 ADU high at blue sites and correctly at red ones, and
            since blue sites are alternate rows AND columns it reached the screen
            as a two-pixel alternation on both axes: fine stripes over the whole
            background at 1:1, 6.4 display levels against 12 of pixel noise, and
            thirty times what MHC or AHD show on the same frame. Invisible to
            every test the suite had, because a demosaic had only ever been
            checked against ITSELF or against the GPU, which mirrors the same
            mistake.
  1a2a70d7  A Bayer mosaic's statistics are three colours, taken on the mosaic in
            place, wherever they are taken; eba30d01 and 3984c741 carry that into
            the live preview and its new histogram.
  "a mosaic's masked luma measured one colour, and the preset cycler stepped off
   a stale index" (#260, cited by subject because it had not landed when this was
   written and a rebase-merge rewrites the hash)
            The masked case was the one still wrong at the end: without a cfa the
            walk is a fixed grid from (0,0) and any EVEN pixelStride keeps both
            parities, so the default 4 never left the phase it started on and the
            star-masked luma measured ONE COLOUR while claiming the whole mosaic.

Viewer - the annotation ladder, and the sky
  a44518e9  The sky behind the frame leaves the O ladder for its own button and
            key (Y). It reads as one more rung of the same idea and is not: it
            puts a second view BEHIND the photograph, takes the pan clamp off and
            raises its own layer palette. Riding the ladder cost it three ways,
            each now pinned: three presses of a key that turned on two layers
            nobody asked for, one mark shared with three other meanings, and on
            an UNSOLVED frame the press did nothing at all, silently, because a
            rung has nowhere to carry a precondition. The button is DIM with a
            reason there instead.
            Y because every letter that reads better is taken twice over: the
            sky's own palette claims G A H C B S O D E M and V the moment the
            backdrop is up, which is exactly when you want to switch it OFF.
  8f817d53  O is the whole annotation, not a rung. The grid-only stop was paid
            for twice per round trip; Shift+O still walks the ladder backwards
            and G toggles the grid on its own.
  02812d12  O adds the objects from the grid rung, and the enhance probe stops
            clipping to black.
  57cc0949  The sky mark becomes the Southern Cross, baked at the sizes it is
            drawn.
  7b769595  Four reports from one sitting with the sky behind the frame.
  66df19e7  The map owns the objects while it is drawn, so nothing is drawn twice.

Viewer - overlay geometry (the correction to the 7.1 listing claim)
  07eb16e3  ComputeScreenPA carried two independent reflections; see the section
            above for the measurement. The star markers were always right while
            the ellipse was not, because markers go through ImageToScreen and the
            ellipse angle did not.
  45ecfa11  The sky map's own ellipse path takes the view's HANDEDNESS. Mirroring
            is applied as a negated position angle, which is exactly what it is,
            so both GPU copies stay byte-identical -- no mirror uniform, no
            shader edit, no .spv re-bake. Scope is the backdrop over a mirrored
            plate, an ordinary frame; the browser atlas and the desktop map were
            never affected.

Viewer - the file list, the display carry, and transport
  a108742b  A click in the file list is its own auto-stretch. The display hold
            shipped ON by default, so every frame of a folder went through the
            curve solved on the first one: on a night that began under a
            light-pollution dome and left it, the galaxy went DIM as the sky got
            darker, the same numbers applied to a lower sky. That is not what a
            click in the list asks for and not what N.I.N.A. or PixInsight show.
            Also Ctrl+O, Ctrl+S (the clean 16-bit raster) and Ctrl+Shift+S (the
            Save button's own menu), all posting to the controller as P and E do.
            TIANWEN_FITS_CACHED_LAYER=0 turns the cached image layer off for one
            run, to attribute a rendering artefact to it or clear it.
  5fe6718b  One rule instead of two: the run's anchor always stands and the SPCC
            triple rides on it, so a click never costs a second fit. The hold
            moved to Ctrl+H, beside Ctrl+Space which returns to the held frame.
            A blink carries exactly what a click does and holds on its own.
  5ef75e48  The held frame carries an [H] in the file list, and Shift+H steps HDR
            back.
  cfea7055  A blink carries within one target, not merely one sensor: two masters
            off the same rig share width, height, planes, depth and CFA whatever
            they are pointed at, so shape called them comparable and a step from
            one target to another was shown with the other one's stretch.

Viewer - colour, white balance and zoom
  40db7943  The colour calibration moves into the white-balance popover, and W
            opens it.
  c2974049  The calibration button says it is calibrating.
  89406ada  Reset is live only when there is something to reset to.
  3553880c  The popover sizes itself to the row it draws.
  bb511853  A colour calibration belongs to the set, and stops following you.
  d23853ae  Measure whether the channels already agree, do not be told.
  7692d1d2  The curve must know about the background neutralisation.
  cffd896c  The boost pivot must know where the sky actually renders.
  69bb9d25  Below 100 percent the colour demosaic runs at the SCREEN's
            resolution, so an OSC frame stops aliasing at fit zoom; 670c5a3a then
            straddles texels rather than fractions of a screen pixel.
  0782be9f  D no longer cycles a demosaic a mono frame has no use for.
  e3cf18a6  An enhance result displays from its own pixels, not those of the
            frame it came from.

Imaging, catalogue and the shell
  974b472f  A dead pixel is not a canvas ring, and auto-crop treated it as one.
            LargestCoveredRectangle called a pixel absent when it was exactly
            zero in every channel, wherever it sat; a canvas ring reaches the
            border by construction, a zero surrounded by data is a pixel some
            calibration clipped. Since this is a largest RECTANGLE and not a
            bounding box, confusing the two is catastrophic rather than slightly
            wrong: measured on IMX455_singleFrame.fits (9576 x 6388), 6230 exact
            zeros, 0.0102 percent of the pixels, made the scan thread between
            them and answer 2922 x 949 -- 4.5 percent of the frame. Under the new
            rule 16 zeros are border-connected and every one lies ON an edge, so
            the crop keeps the frame. NaN stays absence unconditionally.
  715ded46  The thumbnail handler reads nothing until asked and declines a fast
            extraction. A read is what HYDRATES a cloud placeholder, so buffering
            the file in Initialize downloaded a whole folder of frames just
            because Explorer asked whether thumbnails exist -- with every
            resulting picture correct, which is why it went unnoticed.
  1b038589  0x8004B205 is EXTRACTIONPENDING, not a failure; 46bdc75f records why
            a thumbnail was not produced.
  bd1b134e  Inside a OneDrive sync root none of our handler runs at all: a sync
            engine registers ONE whole-root ThumbnailProvider that answers for
            every extension. No per-extension registration can change that.
  5938653b  The catalogue pins OpenNGC to v20260501 and re-bakes both snapshots;
            46f6ee5a fixes the hd-hip-cross bake resolving an input TianWen.Lib
            no longer embeds.
  73f72655  APP's AD-PED is read as the pedestal when PEDESTAL is absent.

KNOWN ISSUES IN THIS BUILD
================================================================================
  P21    Unchanged from 7.1.1627, and still BACKLOG. On a mosaic frame the
         single-channel views show the raw mosaic rather than the separated
         colour planes: ChannelView.DisplayedSourceChannel clamps to the channels
         the IMAGE has, and a 1-channel RGGB frame has one, so Red/Green/Blue all
         resolve to channel 0. Note that the STATISTICS and histogram for such a
         frame ARE three colours as of this release (1a2a70d7); it is the channel
         VIEWS that still show the mosaic, which is a shader change.

  P11.2  Unchanged. The ? panel reports which SAS AI weights are missing and
         where it looked, but cannot fetch them: tools/tianwen-ai-models-fetch.ps1
         is a repo script and a Store install cannot reach it.

  P9/P10 Unchanged, still unconfirmed. A stray text fragment at the left edge,
         and a hand-off that may select the adjacent row. Reported once each,
         neither reproduced since.

  SPCC   Unchanged. Photometric colour calibration is broadband only; narrowband
         is blocked on data rather than maths (per-star Gaia DR3 xp_sampled
         spectra).

  Layout Unchanged. Below about 730 px of window width the image pane arrives at
         zero width and the histogram overlay lands on the file list.

  About  Unchanged, and present in every release so far. The About banner
         concatenates the run attempt onto the version rather than separating it
         (8.0.17161 for 8.0.1716 attempt 1): dotnet.yml composes -p:Version as
         ${VERSION_PREFIX}${VERSION_REV} with no separator, while -p:FileVersion
         is correct.

  CFA    New, and deliberate. The SER frame-by-frame histogram sits a mosaic out
         rather than showing it wrong. It used to pair display channel c with a
         flat walk of the interleaved mosaic under a bound of min(3,1), so every
         colour went into the slot that MEANS red while green and blue kept their
         frame-0 bins -- one wrong curve and two stale ones, all three plausible.
         Closing it means a CFA-aware HistogramDisplay.UpdateRawBins, which is
         backlogged in docs/todo/ui.md.
